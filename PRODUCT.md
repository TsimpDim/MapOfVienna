# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People considering a move to Vienna — researching neighborhoods, costs, landmarks, and quality-of-life factors before deciding where to live.

## Product Purpose

Map of Vienna provides basic data visualisation about the real estate market: district boundaries and names, rough cost indicators, nearby landmarks, rough connections with public transport and keyword summaries per area. It helps potential movers orient themselves across Vienna's districts in a single view.

## Positioning

A district-level real-estate orientation tool for people who are not yet familiar with Vienna — combining geographic, cost, and lifestyle signals in one map surface, rather than scattering them across listing portals and guides.

## Operating Context

Desktop and mobile web browsers. Used during the research phase of a move — before contacting agents or visiting properties.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Interactive map showing Vienna's districts with their names and boundaries
- Rough cost indicators per district (e.g. price ranges, relative affordability)
- Keywords characterizing each district (vibe, demographic, built form)
- Notable landmarks and points of interest per district
- Undecided: data source (OpenStreetMap, custom dataset, third-party API)
- Undecided: level of detail (individual listings vs. district aggregates only)
- Undecided: search, filtering, or comparison features

## Product Principles

1. **Orientation first** — The map is the interface. Every data point is grounded in geography.
2. **Scannable comparison** — Users compare districts at a glance; density of information must not overwhelm clarity.
3. **Honest granularity** — Show district-level aggregates unless real listing data is present. Never simulate individual addresses or prices.
4. **Low-commitment browsing** — The tool is for pre-move research. No accounts, no saved searches, no friction.
